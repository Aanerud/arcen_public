## Forks and modified versions

Forks are welcome, and the licence lets you distribute them, sell them and run
them as a service. What you must do is give your version its own name and its
own branding:

- choose a name that is not "Arcen" and is not confusingly similar to it;
- replace the application names, icons and bundle display names before you
  distribute builds;
- do not suggest that your version is official, endorsed by or affiliated with
  TUTTIFRUTTI AS.

The source tree uses "Arcen" in identifiers, file names, bundle identifiers
and protocol strings. You may keep those internal identifiers unchanged where
they are needed for compatibility; what matters is the name and branding your
users see.

## Allowed nominative use

You may use the names in plain text to refer truthfully to Arcen, for example:

- "based on Arcen";
- "a fork of Arcen";
- "compatible with Arcen Pier";
- "works with Arcen Deck".

Keep such references factual, in ordinary text rather than in a logo-like
style, and do not make them the name of your product.

## Building and using it yourself

You may share the source code, including its names, exactly as published.
Building it yourself and running the result — at home, in a studio, or across
a whole organisation — is always fine, even though that build shows the name
Arcen. The restrictions above apply when you distribute, sell or host builds,
images or services for others.
