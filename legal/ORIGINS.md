# Origins

Arcen is original work by its copyright holder. It grew out of earlier
first-party prototype work by the same author, which was relicensed by that
author under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 when this repository was
opened. There is no third-party copyright in the first-party source tree.

## Inspiration

Arcen was informed by the published behaviour of existing remote-desktop and
pixel-streaming systems — the general shape of the problem, the vocabulary of
the field, and the performance bar those systems set. That influence is at the
level of ideas, protocol concepts, and prior art.

No third-party source code, proprietary SDK payload, decompiled binary,
reference corpus, or vendor implementation was copied, ported, or derived into
this repository.

## Third-party components

Arcen links and bundles third-party open-source components. Each retains its own
licence, and every one of them is recorded with its full notice in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). That file is exhaustive by
policy: if a dependency ships in a release artefact, its notice belongs there.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the AGPL-3.0 only. There is no contributor
licence agreement and no copyright assignment, because there is no proprietary
edition for the copyright to be assigned into — Arcen is free software and will
stay that way. By opening a pull request you agree that your contribution is
licensed under AGPL-3.0 and that you have the right to submit it.
